02 — What Comes Next

The admin — so you can run it yourself.

Right now the site has a basic admin page at /admin where you can add entries and update your book details. It works — but it saves data only in your browser, which means if you switch to a different device, your changes aren't there.

Before launch, we'll connect the admin to a secure cloud database (free tier — zero monthly cost). Once that's done, you can add a new essay from your phone at a café and it goes live on the site immediately.

What the upgraded admin will do
All of this from a clean, password-protected page — no technical knowledge needed.
1
Write a new entry — title, date, category, body. Hit publish. It's live.
2

3
Update the book — change the price, paste in your Chapter 1 excerpt, toggle signed copies on or off.
4
Track orders & readers — See customer names, phone numbers, delivery locations, order statuses, and your full email subscriber list in one place.
5
Works from any device — phone, laptop, borrowed computer. Your content and customer records live securely in the cloud.
